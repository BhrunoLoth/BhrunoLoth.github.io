Email Vault: secure operations and traceable backend workflows

A backend-focused case study covering access control, email infrastructure integration, database design and automated verification.

NestJSTypeScriptPostgreSQLPrismaDockerJWT / TOTPAPI testing

Problem and engineering approach

Scoped access

Coordinate multiple authorized mailboxes without distributing provider credentials to every operator. Distinguish operator access from account-level authorization.

Traceability

Record who initiated an action, its target, when it occurred and its outcome. Treat auditability as a core application concern, not a reporting afterthought.

Service integration

Separate external email infrastructure, authentication logic, permissions and business operations into independently testable backend responsibilities.

Design and validation

Identity and permissions

  • JWT-based sessions and second-factor authentication concepts
  • Per-operator and per-account permission checks
  • Separation of privileged administration and routine operations

Reliability

  • Structured API validation and consistent error handling
  • Prisma data access and schema evolution
  • Automated unit and end-to-end testing
